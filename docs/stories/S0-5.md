# S0-5: Turn epics E1–E5 into stories and plan Sprint 1

**Epic:** Sprint 0 foundations · **Size:** M · **Owner:** Project Manager · **Status:** Backlog

**Dependencies:** S0-1 (data-source verdicts), S0-2 (OpenAPI v0, ADR-003), S0-4 (oracle format), product owner approval of data sources (D-08) and TCO model v0.2 (D-09).
**Blocks:** Sprint 1 start.

## Story
As the product owner, I want the epics broken into small, sized stories with a proposed Sprint 1, so that I can approve the next demoable increment.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given epics E1–E5 in `docs/03-initial-backlog.md`, When S0-5 is done, Then `docs/stories/` contains stories from the template for each epic, each with Given/When/Then acceptance criteria, a size (S/M/L), an owner and dependencies.
- Given the data-source verdicts from S0-1, When E1/E2 stories are written, Then no story depends on a rejected source, and stories that depend on a fallback (e.g. manual fuel-price config) say so.
- Given the TCO model v0.2, When E3 stories are written, Then they reference the oracle cases from S0-4 and cover PHEV, used cars (`A0`, odometer `O0`), the residual value as the lower of the age curve and the km line (`Kmax` per powertrain), the cumulative cost `C(y)` per year, TCO per year and cost per km. Financing and break-even are out of v1 and get no stories.
- Given all stories, When Sprint 1 is proposed in `status/sprint-01-plan.md`, Then it has a goal, a demoable outcome in `prod`, stories in execution order, and only stories that are Ready (design questions answered) are assigned to the Developer.
- Given ideas outside `docs/01-product-brief.md`, When they come up, Then they go to `docs/parking-lot.md`, not into Sprint 1.

## Design notes (Architect)
- ADRs / contract sections affected: none. Architect reviews the E1–E3 stories for design gaps before Sprint 1 starts.

## Test notes (Tester)
- Oracle cases / edge cases: Tester reviews that E3/E4 acceptance criteria are testable.

## Out of scope
- Writing stories for v2 items (company-car tax, more countries, more than two cars).
